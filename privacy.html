Privacy and sign-in

Your account credentials stay on your computer or go to Microsoft's authentication services. Optional cape sharing and Discord presence send limited information when you enable them. This website has no analytics.

Last updated 4 October 2026

Who makes Goon Client

Goon Client is a free, non-commercial Minecraft: Java Edition client and launcher made by Goon Nation, a group of friends who play together. It has no ads, no paid features and no analytics.

How signing in works

Goon Client uses Microsoft's standard sign-in for devices. You never type your password into Goon Client.

  1. The launcher shows a short code and opens microsoft.com/link in your browser.
  2. You enter the code and sign in on Microsoft's own page. Microsoft asks you to allow two permissions: XboxLive.signin (sign in to Xbox Live, which Minecraft uses) and offline_access (stay signed in so you don't have to do this every time).
  3. The launcher swaps Microsoft's token for an Xbox Live token, then for a Minecraft token, the same way the official Minecraft Launcher does.
  4. It reads your Minecraft profile (name, UUID and skin) to check that the account owns Minecraft: Java Edition. Accounts that don't own the game can't play.

Until Mojang approves Goon Client's app for the Minecraft API, pressing Play hands you over to the official Minecraft Launcher, which signs you in instead. Goon Client doesn't see or store anything in that case.

What's stored on your computer

Everything lives in Goon Client's own folder, %APPDATA%\.goonclient. These files are stored locally; optional connected features are described below.

FileWhat's in it
account.jsonYour Minecraft name, UUID and skin link, the Minecraft access token and the Microsoft refresh token that keeps you signed in. Never your password. Signing out deletes this file.
launcher.jsonLauncher settings, such as how much RAM the game gets.
config/goonclient/Which modules are on, their settings, HUD positions and your waypoints.
game-files/Minecraft itself, its libraries and assets, downloaded from Mojang.

Once the game is running, Minecraft connects to Mojang and Microsoft services as it normally would (skins, multiplayer sign-in, Realms) and to the servers you choose to join. Optional connected features in the Goon Client mod are described below.

Optional connected features

Cape sharing: off by default and inactive until the owner configures a cosmetics service. If you turn on Share with friends, the service receives your Minecraft name, UUID, selected cape ID and an account-verification challenge. Looking up other players’ capes sends their UUIDs to that service. Your Minecraft access token goes to Mojang for the verification step, never to the cape service. The service host may also record normal connection information such as IP addresses.

Discord presence: off by default. When enabled, Goon Client passes generic menu, singleplayer or multiplayer activity and an optional elapsed timer to your desktop Discord app. Discord can display this on your profile. Server addresses and Minecraft account credentials are not included.

Updates: the launcher checks the configured update website for a release manifest and downloads the pack when you choose to update. The host receives normal web requests. Your sign-in tokens are not sent to the update website.

This website

goonnation.xyz is a plain static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. The only thing it saves is the accent colour you pick, in your own browser. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Signing out and removing access

  • Press Sign out in the launcher to delete the saved tokens from your computer.
  • To revoke Goon Client's access completely, remove it from your Microsoft account's app permissions at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • To remove everything, delete the %APPDATA%\.goonclient folder and the launcher.
