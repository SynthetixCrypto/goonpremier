A few things
before you play.

Setup, servers and your account.

How do I install Goon Client?

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the whole folder and run Goon Client Launcher.exe. Select the official Minecraft Launcher option, then choose the Goon Client installation. You need a Minecraft Java account.

How do I get the HUD from the screenshots?

Open Settings > Configs > Combatant in game. Use the HUD editor to move and resize the panels afterward.

Can I use it on a server?

Check the server’s rules. Some optional overlays, radar and building assists may be restricted. Keep those disabled where they aren’t allowed.

Does it support shaders and schematics?

Yes. Iris and Litematica are included. Choose your own shader pack, and enable Schematics when you want to use it. The stick tool starts off.

Can my friends see my cape?

Friends using Goon Client can see shared cosmetics when the community service is running and sharing is enabled. Your local cape works without sharing.

What happens to my account information?

Sign-in uses Microsoft’s own website. Tokens stay on your PC and go only to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft. Read the privacy details.

A new look, top to bottom
  • New Goon Client logo: a glass tile with a rounded-square G in your theme colour.
  • Nation Noir cape, and every cape redrawn in the new style with the Goon mark.

  • Real frosted glass: the world behind the HUD, hotbar and menus is blurred live.
  • Settings, pause menu, main menu and Wardrobe use the see-through glass.
  • HUD editor lists every setting of the selected element.
  • Potions, Keybinds, Cooldowns, Inventory, target card and notifications match Combatant's layout.
  • Theme colours are Combatant's exact ones (Noir pink is a little brighter).
  • Built-in setups only switch modules now; your theme and HUD layout stay put.
  • Discord status works on Windows again and shows the right symbols.
  • Goon Client is now GPL-3.0; the launcher includes the source and credits Combatant.
  • Bundled mods no longer pin exact versions; EntityCulling is now an optional separate install.
